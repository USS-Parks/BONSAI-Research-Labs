# Discovery-cycle health metrics

BK-11 measures forward rates, backward-credit latency and magnitude, survival by utility, generations, bottlenecks, useful/total growth, maintenance versus benefit, collapse, runaway growth, cycling, and ossification.

Artifact count is not open-endedness. Open-endedness is novelty among the population; total size is used only to detect collapse or runaway growth.

The committed fixture at `fixtures/cycle-health/v1/expected-outcomes.json` separates healthy, collapse, runaway, cycling, and ossified traces.
