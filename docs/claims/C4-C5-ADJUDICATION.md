# C4 and C5 adjudication

BV-06 requires forward construction plus backward credit across at least three families for C4, with multiplicity correction and no comparator mixing. C5 requires C4, at least two generations, net gain, energy at least E2, and no uncontrolled growth.

One-score, one-seed, one-family, artifact-count, and scaled-compute shortcuts fail. Missing energy fails C5. M3 does not require a C4/C5 pass.
